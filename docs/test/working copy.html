Take, for example, the just over 1,000 people who were booked in January 2025.

Over the next 12 months, over 40% of them were booked again.

Almost half of those booked again were also charged with drug possession or use among their charges.



Most people were booked for multiple charges each time they were arrested, and these charges aren’t always reflective of a new crime. For instance, someone may be booked if they have outstanding warrants for failing to appear in court on previous charges.

Two out of every three were booked for the same alleged crime later in the year.
